UKRAINE — KRYVYI RIH

Triple-tap strike kills a boy of seven in Kryvyi Rih

Missiles hit rescuers as they arrived; 60 wounded in Zelensky’s home city.
Russian missiles struck a restaurant car park in Kryvyi Rih on Friday night, then drones hit the rescue workers who answered the call — and a third strike followed minutes later. A seven-year-old boy and a 20-year-old paramedic were killed and nearly 60 people were wounded, among them six children.
THE GULF — HORMUZ

Iran hits Bahrain and tankers as Gulf war widens

Two tankers struck in Hormuz; a Bahrain plant burns; Tehran claims a US drone down.
Iran struck a civilian industrial site in Bahrain on Saturday, setting it ablaze, and two tankers were hit by projectiles in the Strait of Hormuz, with no casualties reported. Tehran said it had shot down a US drone over Qeshm Island, and Qatar sent mediators to Tehran.
CLIMATE — COP31, ANTALYA

Climate summit runs out of time in Antalya

“A good deal or no deal,” the EU warns, as fossil fuels vanish from the draft.
COP31 stretched deep into overtime on Saturday, past its Friday deadline, with nearly 200 countries deadlocked over a text that drops fossil fuels and a $120bn finance target. The EU’s climate chief said the summit would produce a good deal or none at all.
SAUDI ARABIA — RIYADH

Houthis fire on Riyadh after Saudi strikes

Three missiles intercepted over the capital.
Yemen’s Houthis fired on Riyadh on Saturday, vowing revenge for a Saudi-led “special operation” on Hodeida. The coalition said it intercepted three ballistic missiles; two more were stopped over Jizan.
BRAZIL — RIO GRANDE DO SUL

Storm flattens a Brazilian town

Four dead, 162 hurt; 8,000 homes damaged in Tupanciretã.
A supercell storm tore through Tupanciretã in 40 minutes before dawn on Friday, with 114 km/h winds. Nearly all 20,000 residents were forced from their homes; the mayor said “the town is gone.”
SCIENCE — MATHEMATICS

AI firm worker claims maths’ greatest prize

A proof of the Riemann Hypothesis — posted, not yet verified.
An Anthropic mathematician says he has proved the Riemann Hypothesis with the help of the company’s AI, Claude, posting a proof in 17 million lines of Lean code. The Clay Institute still lists the 167-year-old problem as unsolved.
GERMANY — INDUSTRY

German metalworkers to strike on Monday

IG Metall calls out 23,000 workers in a pay fight.
Germany’s IG Metall union said on Sunday it would launch a 24-hour strike on Monday, calling 23,000 workers at 19 firms in the north out in an escalating pay dispute.
